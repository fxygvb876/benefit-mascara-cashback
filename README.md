### Which one you actually want

**Straight, downward-pointing lashes:** Roller Lash. The curve in the wand hooks lashes upward at the root rather than just coating them, which is why people with stubbornly straight lashes tend to report more lift from this than from volumizing formulas. The trade-off, noted in Who What Wear's side-by-side test, is that it's less dramatic than the volume-first tubes. It's a daytime mascara that looks like your lashes, only better behaved.

**False-lash drama:** BADgal BANG!. This is the one to pick if you want visible bulk without extensions. The thin brush reaches the inner corner and lower lashes, and the aero-particle formula is genuinely long-wearing — Benefit rates it for up to 36 hours. It can clump if you keep layering past three coats, which is the single most common complaint in editor reviews.

**Fluffy, blown-out volume:** BADgal Bounce. The hybrid brush is the whole point here. You load up with the fibre side, then rotate to the comb side to separate. Benefit's own testing measured +242% more volume after three coats, and a self-evaluation of 116 participants found 93% said lashes looked fuller, fluffier and instantly lifted. Independent reviews are more mixed: OK! found the dry formula transfer-resistant but not very lengthening, and the double-sided brush takes a few applications to click. Macy's customer reviews include people who found it heavy or that it migrated to the eyelid.

**Fanned out, natural but defined:** Fan Fest. One coat reads natural, three coats read full, and the fibre brush separates lashes instead of clumping them together. Several editors name it their favourite in the whole range. It's also the one most often described as "not enough volume" if you're used to BADgal BANG!.

**One tube to rule them all:** They're Real!. It volumizes, lengthens and curls without being the best at any single thing. If you don't want to think about your lash type at 7 a.m., that's the argument for it.

## Mini or full size?

The math is closer than the price tags suggest. A full-size tube is 8.5 g for $29 — roughly $3.41 per gram. A mini runs about 4 g for $16, or $4 per gram. So per gram, full size wins by around 15%, though you're spending nearly double up front.

Per gram isn't the right lens for most people, though. Mascara goes off long before you scrape the tube clean, and the practical shelf life after opening is about three months. If you normally toss a tube half-used, the mini is the better buy even at the worse rate — you're paying $16 to use 90% of it instead of $29 to use half. Minis are also how you test whether BADgal Bounce's dry, fluffy formula is for you before committing to a full size, and they're the reason lash-per-gram arguments get overthought.

The exception: gift sets. Ulta currently lists a Lit Up Lashes Mini Mascara Trio — BADgal BANG!, Fan Fest and Roller Lash minis — at $34 against a stated $48 value, and Benefit's four-piece mini sets have bounced between $32 and $45 depending on the season. If you want to try two or three formulas rather than gamble on one, sets are where the per-tube price actually breaks.

## Questions people ask before buying

**Is Benefit mascara worth $29?** If you wear mascara daily and care about how it performs, yes for most people — they're genuinely good formulas, and the brand's mascara dominance isn't just marketing. If you go through a tube a year, no: a mini at $16 will outlast your usage and cost less.

**Which Benefit mascara is best for short lashes?** Roller Lash for lift and curl, They're Real! Magnet for length. BADgal BANG! adds bulk but the slim brush matters more for coating small lashes than for extending them.

**Do I need the waterproof version?** Only if you're swimming, crying through a wedding, or have extremely oily lids. The standard formulas already hold up through a workday, and waterproof is harder to remove.

**Can I use cashback and a sale price together?** Yes — sale prices are what cashback is calculated on, so a $14.50 tube during a half-off event earns less cashback in absolute terms but you pay half as much. Stacking works; it just doesn't multiply.

**How long until I get the money?** 90 days for Benefit, 120 days for Ulta, plus PayPal transfer time. Plan for a season, not a weekend.
